Playing

How do I attack?
Tap one of your guests, then tap an enemy guest. Roll above their defence and they're defeated. Full rules are in the in-game menu.
Why can't my guest attack?
Usually because they were played this turn, you've already used your turn on a card, or an enemy Taunt guest must be dealt with first. The game tells you which.
My deck is stuck at the wrong number of cards.
Use Reset Game on the home screen to start a fresh career.

Your data

Open the Leaderboard and choose Delete my leaderboard data.
