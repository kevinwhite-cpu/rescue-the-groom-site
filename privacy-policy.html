Rescue the Groom — Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 September 2026

Rescue the Groom is a small card game made for the guests of one wedding. It is not a business, it shows no advertising, and it sells nothing. This policy explains exactly what the app does with information.

What is stored on your device

Your card collection, Wedding Points, win/loss record, streak, favourite cards, chosen dice colour and the friends you have added are all saved on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

What leaves your device

Only one thing: your leaderboard entry. When you finish a game the app sends the display name you chose, your score, career victories, best streak, number of cards collected, and your win and loss counts to our leaderboard, so players can be ranked against each other. Nothing else is transmitted.

To do this the app signs you in anonymously. That creates a random identifier so your row can be updated as you play. It is not linked to your Apple ID, your email address, your phone number or your name unless you type your own name into the display-name field. You choose that name, and you can change it at any time.

The leaderboard is hosted on Google Firebase, which stores this data on our behalf and provides the anonymous sign-in. Google's handling of it is covered by their privacy policy.

Contacts and friends

The app never reads your address book. When you add a friend, iOS shows you its own contact picker and hands back only the single person you select; the app cannot see anyone else. That friend's name and number are saved on your device so you can send them an invitation. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Removing a friend in the app deletes them from your device.

Deleting your data

Open the Leaderboard and choose Delete my leaderboard data. This permanently erases your leaderboard row and the anonymous account behind it. It cannot be undone. To remove everything else, use Reset career or delete the app.

Children

The app has no chat, no messaging, no advertising, no purchases and no user-generated content beyond a display name on the leaderboard.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and in the app.
