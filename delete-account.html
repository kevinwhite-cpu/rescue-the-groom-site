Rescue the Groom: Delete your account

Rescue the Groom, the wedding trading card game, by Kevin White.

You can delete your player profile and everything stored with it at any time, from inside the app. It takes a few seconds and nobody has to approve it.

In the app

  1. Open Rescue the Groom and tap Leaderboard on the home screen.
  2. Choose Delete my leaderboard data.
  3. Confirm it is you, with Google on an Android phone or with Apple on an iPhone or iPad.

The deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record, including the email address Google shares when you sign in.
  • Your leaderboard entry: display name, score, career victories, best streak, card count, wins and losses.
  • The backup of your career: the cards you own, your favourites, points, record and streak.
  • The one-way code (hash) made from your mobile number.
  • Your friendships, invitations and friend match records.
  • The notification token for your phone.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our side, and there is no retention period. Your cards remain on the phone you are holding, because they are saved on the device itself. To remove those too, use Reset career in the app, or delete the app.

Can't open the app?

Email kgw209@gmail.com from any address, tell us the display name you used, and your account and its data will be deleted for you, normally within a few days.
